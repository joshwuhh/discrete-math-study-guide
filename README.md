# Discrete Mathematics I — Interactive Study Guide

A self-contained, mobile-first study guide for Discrete Mathematics I. No login, no build step — one `index.html`.

## What's inside

| # | Competency | Weight | Exam Q | Bank Q |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logic & Proofs | ~22% | 11 | 33 |
| 2 | Sets & Functions | ~16% | 8 | 24 |
| 3 | Boolean Algebra | ~14% | 7 | 21 |
| 4 | Matrix Operations | ~8% | 4 | 12 |
| 5 | Finite & Infinite Series | ~12% | 6 | 18 |
| 6 | Relations | ~12% | 6 | 18 |
| 7 | Graphs & Trees | ~16% | 8 | 24 |

- Concept explanations, 95 definition cards with real-world example toggles, worked examples, and 78 embedded practice Q&A
- 1-week study plan ordered by topic weight; coverage progress bar (saved per device)
- Quick-reference page with search and flashcard mode
- **Mock exam:** 150-question bank → 50-question stratified draw, shuffled options, swipe cards, progress dots, explanations, topic breakdown, attempt history with weak areas, review mode

## Install on iPhone
Open the site in Safari → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.

Weights are estimates used to size the mock exam; check the course page for any official breakdown.
